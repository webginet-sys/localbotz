Talk & Live Voice

Talking is the heart of this mission. Everyone should be able to talk to their computer and get things done — no keyboard, no menus, no carefully worded prompt.

Get LocalBotz free

Talk — voice in, voice out

Tap the mic in chat and speak in plain words. The browser types what you said and sends it as a normal chat message. Turn the speaker on and the bot's replies are read out loud. No install, no keys, no audio recorded by LocalBotz.

✓

Plain-language rule. When Talk is on, the bot replies in short plain sentences, no jargon, no menus. If something is unclear, it asks ONE simple question. It never asks you to write a better prompt.

✓

Your language. Talk follows the language you picked — per bot, per chat, or for the whole app. 199 countries, 186 languages.

✓

Honest when it can't. A browser with no speech recognition says so when you tap Talk, and never crashes the chat. Talk needs Chrome or Edge; the speaker works in most browsers.

✓

Optional local transcription. Upload an audio file and LocalBotz transcribes it on your computer — only if you installed the optional transcriber. Without it, you get an honest "not installed" instead of a fake transcript.

Show-me-how — visible steps on your screen

Flip the toggle and the bot still does the work — it just shows each step: what it is about to click or type and where, in plain words, with a picture after each desktop or browser step. Off, it does it quietly. On, it shows every step. Same bot, same work.

Live Voice — a real conversation

Talk is turn by turn. Live Voice is live: tap Start, talk in plain words, and the bot answers out loud while you speak. Interrupt it any time — just start talking — and it can do things on the desktop when you ask ("look at the BOQ", "click the total").

Live

Interrupt any time

Tap Interrupt, or just start talking while it speaks. Playback stops at once, the model is told to stop, and it waits for what you say next.

Live

It stops itself

Say nothing for 2 minutes and the session stops, the mic closes, and the words — text only, no audio — are saved into the bot's chat history.

Live

Powered by Gemini Live

Live voice runs on Google Gemini Live, so it needs internet and a Gemini key, and usage is billed to the key owner. Without a key the page says so honestly and refuses to start. The local model is text-only and cannot do live audio.

Where this is going

Questions people ask

Is any audio saved?

No. Audio is streamed and discarded. Only the text transcript is kept, in the bot's chat history. Every live session is logged as start/stop, duration, turns and interruptions — no audio.

Can live voice use the desktop?

Yes — it can inspect, click and type, through the same permission gate as every other tool. Desktop control off, or a bot on Ask, blocks clicking and typing. Every live desktop action is logged.

How long can a live session run?

Gemini caps a live audio session at about 15 minutes. The page ends the session when Gemini does, and the transcript is still saved.
