Three ways to run a business on it

The owner supplies the keys and the rules. The team just works.

1

Master Managed Fixed

The owner supplies the master keys and assigns one fixed default model. Tenants do nothing — zero config — and usage is metered to their workspace wallet.

2

Master User-Selectable

The owner supplies the master keys and curates the allowed model roster. Tenants pick from the roster. No one needs their own keys.

3

BYOK

Tenants bring their own keys. The host pays nothing for API usage — local models and the tenants' own keys do the work.

✓

Local models are always free

In every mode, the local model costs the host nothing and is never metered.

Questions people ask

Do I need my own API keys?

No. In the two Master modes the owner supplies the keys and the team just works. In BYOK mode each tenant brings their own. And the local model needs no key at all.

Who sees my team's work?

Your office, its memory and its files live on your computer — or on the rented server you registered on. Nothing is shared between workspaces, and usage is logged as counts and costs only, never message content.

What does the Cloud Office cost?

The server owner sets the Cloud Office monthly price in the admin panel. Until it is set, the panel shows that the price is not set yet — we don't invent one here.
