Questions people ask

Does it work without internet?

Yes — with the local model. LM Studio and Qwen3.5 run entirely on your computer, so chat, office jobs and the workroom work offline. Online providers need internet, and the chat says so plainly when they are not reachable.

Does it generate images or videos?

No. LocalBotz is for office, sales and marketing work — chat, documents, deals, research, automation. Image and video generation are deliberately out of scope.

Where does my data live?

On your computer, in plain files under the data folder — bots, memory, offices, workrooms, documents. You choose the folder at install time (C: drive or D: drive), and you can back it up or delete it yourself.
