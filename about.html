About Us

LocalBotz gives you a team of AI specialists that think as one — on your own computer, with your own models, and yours to run.

What LocalBotz is

A personal AI assistant that lives on your computer. Your local model, plus Claude, GPT, Gemini and DeepSeek — building and automating your work in one agent. Free, private by design, and open source.

✓

Local first. The default model runs on your computer. Your chats, files and memory stay there unless you choose an online model.

✓

A team, not a chatbot. Ask the team a question and it pools what every member knows: independent first takes, options backed by evidence, and a decision you can follow line by line.

✓

For office, sales and marketing. Quotations, tenders, BOQs, deals, follow-ups, posts — the work companies actually do, in every sector.

✓

AI suggests, humans decide. Every job is checked by an independent Auditor and stays a draft until you approve it. Nothing is sent until you say so.

✓

Open source. MIT licensed, built in the open, on GitHub. Read the code, run it from source, or send a pull request.

✓

Talking is the heart of it. Everyone should be able to talk to their computer and get things done — no prompting, no tutorials before you can start. Our culture →

Who makes it

LocalBotz is a product of WEBGINET ENTERPRISE PRIVATE LIMITED

Built for the companies we work with — construction, MEP, electrical engineering, IT services and e-commerce — and for anyone who wants their AI team on their own computer.

What LocalBotz is not

Saying no is part of the product. LocalBotz does not generate images or videos, does not fine-tune models, does not run game bots, and does not wrap other companies' terminal tools as its own.
