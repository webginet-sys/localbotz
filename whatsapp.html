WhatsApp sales agent

Customers message your WhatsApp Business number, LocalBotz drafts a reply, and nothing is sent until you approve it. The same rule as the Auditor: a draft is a draft until a human says send.

Get LocalBotz free

How it works

1

Customer writes in

A customer writes to your WhatsApp Business number. Meta calls LocalBotz, and the call is checked — the signature must match, or the message is rejected.

2

Only approved senders

Only senders on your allowlist get a reply. Anyone else is logged as blocked and gets nothing. A do-not-reply blocklist always wins.

3

A draft, not a send

Your WhatsApp bot drafts a reply, using your business profile and the rule never to invent a price, a date or a delivery. The draft waits in your inbox.

4

You approve it

Approve it in the panel and only then is it sent, through the official WhatsApp Business API. A failed send is reported honestly, and the message stays waiting.

Two ways to run it

Hosted

On the server

The gateway-hosted agent lives on the VPS. Set it up in the user panel — connect form, webhook URL, inbox with Approve & send — and it works even when your computer is off. The admin panel sees every workspace's status.

Local

On your computer

The LocalBotz app on your computer has its own WhatsApp agent too, on the Messaging channels page. Same rule: draft first, approve, then send.

Setup

What you need

A WhatsApp Business number and a Meta app: access token, phone number ID, verify token, app secret. They live in the Secure store, masked everywhere, never logged.

Official API only

There is no QR-code pairing: logging in by scanning a code breaks WhatsApp's rules and can get the number banned, so LocalBotz does not do it. Text messages only — voice notes, images and status updates are ignored.
