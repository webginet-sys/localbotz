Workroom — documents with proof

A vault of project documents for one job. Ask a question, get the answer — with the exact document and passage it came from. Never a made-up price, measurement or date.

How it works

1

Put the documents in

BOQs, tenders, contracts, SOWs, SLAs, invoices, correspondence. Upload them, or add them from the workspace folder. They stay on your computer.

2

Search them

Keyword search over the documents, in passages. A big document set is searched as the work happens, not stuffed into one prompt.

3

Ask with receipts

The model only sees the matching passages, and is told to answer only from them. Every answer cites its document and passage.

4

Hand it over

Export one package — the workroom, all documents, every question and answer with its sources, the redaction log and the reviewer notes — for the next reviewer.

Private by design

✓

Redaction before online models. Emails, IBANs, card numbers and your own private terms are replaced with placeholders before anything leaves the computer — and swapped back into the answer on your computer.

✓

Local models skip redaction. LM Studio and Ollama never leave the computer, so redaction is off for them unless you ask for it.

✓

The record keeps no secrets. The redaction log stores only what kind of thing was hidden and how many — never the values themselves.

✓

Honest about what it cannot read. A file whose text cannot be extracted is still stored, and the workroom says so on the document instead of pretending it searched it.

.txt, .md, .csv, .json, .log, .tsv, .pdf and .docx — up to 20 MB each. A scanned-image PDF has no text to extract, and the workroom says so.

Questions people ask

Is the search semantic?

Not yet — search is keyword-based, so a question in very different words from the document may miss. Use the document's own words (for example "retention" or "payment terms") for the best matches.

Does it upload my documents anywhere?

No. Documents stay under the workroom folder on your computer. Only the matching passages are sent to a model — and to a local model, nothing leaves at all.
