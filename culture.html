Culture

Computers were supposed to work for us. Somewhere along the way, we started working for them. LocalBotz exists to turn that back around.

Talking is the heart of this mission

AI was supposed to end the drudgery. Instead it added one more thing to learn: how to prompt it. That is backwards. Everyone should be able to talk to their computer and get things done — no keyboard, no menus, no carefully worded prompt. You just say what you want. They ask, and it shows them how — on their own screen, in their own context, in the moment. No prompting. No complicated steps. No tutorials before you can start.

Many people are not perfect at typing, and they are used to talking on calls. So the future we are building toward is our own OS and our own mobile app with push-to-talk — so all our AI agents work from them and reply back in a voice, easy to talk to like employer and employee, and feeling like they are in the office. That is the plan for later. Today, Talk and Live Voice in the app are the start of it.

How we work

✓

Plain words win. If a feature needs a tutorial to use, it is not finished. The bot answers in short plain sentences, and shows each step on your screen.

✓

AI suggests, humans decide. The Auditor checks, the draft waits, and nothing is sent until a person says so. A machine that acts without asking is not helpful — it is a liability.

✓

Private by design. Your work stays on your computer unless you choose otherwise. A product that quietly uploads your business is not a product we would use ourselves.

✓

Honest about limits. If something was only tested in mock mode, we say so. If a model cannot answer, the app says so instead of pretending. Trust is built by being wrong loudly, not by hiding it.

✓

Build it, then write it down. A feature ships, is tested, and gets a guide with its limits. Work that is not written down does not exist yet.

✓

Learn from everyone, copy no one. Public projects are read for ideas — then LocalBotz is written from scratch, under its own name, in its own words.
